💡 How Proportional Splitting Works

Instead of splitting service charge equally (which is unfair if one friend ordered a £4 tea and another had a £40 steak), we split service charge proportionally based on food & drink spend:

Person's Service Charge = (Person's Food Subtotal ÷ Bill Subtotal) × Total Service Charge

For Shared Items: If two friends share a £14 Pizza, £7 goes to Friend A and £7 goes to Friend B. Each pays service charge only on their £7 share.

This guarantees that every penny of the tip is distributed fairly and sums up exactly to the restaurant's receipt.
